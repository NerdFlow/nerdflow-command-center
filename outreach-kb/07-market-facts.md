# 07 — Market Facts and Proof Points

**Purpose:** Give the sales agent statistics it can trust, with the source, publisher, year and a quality rating for each, so outreach is persuasive without being misleading.

**Source-quality ratings**
- **Independent/government:** BLS, Google's own policy statements, academic/HBR research.
- **Industry body/publication:** trade press, M&A advisors, consumer cost guides built on project data (Angi), and large field-service software datasets (ServiceTitan, Samsara, Jobber, Housecall Pro). **Caution:** ServiceTitan, Jobber and Housecall Pro now sell their own AI receptionist / call-answering features (see Competitors), so their call and booking data is **vendor data from competitors**. Use it only with the publisher named ("ServiceTitan's data shows..."), never as an independent fact.
- **Vendor marketing (use with caution):** published by companies that sell call tracking, answering services or AI receptionists (Invoca, CallRail, PATLive, Moneypenny, AMBS, AI-receptionist blogs, LSA agencies).

**Rules**
1. Only quote stats from the **Safe to quote** section, using the phrasing given there. Always name the source in the sentence ("according to BLS...", "CallRail's 2025 survey of 1,000 consumers found...").
2. Never present a vendor stat as an independent fact.
3. Never quote anything on the **Do not quote** list, even if the prospect's own website uses it.
4. Never turn a market average into a claim about the prospect's own business ("you are losing $X"). Use "businesses like yours" or ask them for their numbers.
5. Round honestly. Do not upgrade "about 1 in 4" to "1 in 3".
6. Numbers marked **(unverified)** may inform your thinking but must not appear in outreach.

## 3. Value of a call: average job/ticket by trade

All of the following come from Angi or ConsumerAffairs consumer cost guides. They are averages of homeowner-reported or project data, rated **Industry body/publication**. Use them as "a typical repair runs about...", not as the prospect's ticket.

| Trade / job | Typical cost | Emergency / after-hours premium | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| HVAC repair | Avg **~$350**; range $100–$3,000; service call $100–$250 | After-hours adds **$40–$80/hr** | Angi 2026, https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-hvac-repair-cost.htm |
| Plumbing repair | Avg **~$341**; range $99–$990; service call $100–$250 | Emergency service **1.5–3x** standard rates; **+$300–$400** for after-hours/weekends/holidays | Angi 2026, https://www.angi.com/articles/plumber-cost.htm |
| Electrician | $50–$130/hr; first hour $100–$200 for quick jobs | Emergency/after-hours **about doubles** standard rates | ConsumerAffairs 2026, https://www.consumeraffairs.com/homeowners/how-much-does-an-electrician-cost.html |
| Garage door repair | Avg **~$265**; range $80–$700+; springs $180–$350 | Not quantified by source | Angi 2026, https://www.angi.com/articles/most-common-garage-door-repair-costs.htm |
| Roof repair | Avg **~$1,174**; typical $395–$1,967 | Emergency fee **+$100–$300** | Angi 2026, https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-do-roof-repairs-cost.htm |
| Water damage restoration | Avg **~$3,868**; typical $1,383–$6,369 | Not separately quantified | Angi 2026, https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-it-cost-repair-water-damage.htm |
| Pest control (one-time) | Not verified in this research | — | (unverified). Do not quote. |

**Notes**
- Replacement and install jobs (e.g. HVAC system, roof replacement) are far larger. Invoca's illustrative furnace replacement is $4,500 (vendor example, not a benchmark). A single answered call can lead to these jobs, but don't claim any particular call would.
- For a quick value model, use: *missed calls/month × booking rate × average ticket*. ServiceTitan booking-rate data (2022, 3,000+ businesses) shows a **42%** average (plumbing 43%, electrical 41%, HVAC 38%, garage door 31%), and **24%** for shops with fewer than 5 techs. Evening calls after 6pm book at only **21%** for large shops and **9%** for small ones. Source: https://www.servicetitan.com/blog/data-call-booking-rates (Industry body/publication). ServiceTitan's 2026 data shows top performers book **62%** of inbound lead calls versus **39%** for the rest: https://www.servicetitan.com/toolbox/state-of-the-trades/trends/top-performer-profile
- Example (internal reasoning only, do not present as their loss): 20 missed calls/month × 38% booking × $350 = about $2,660/month in HVAC repair revenue at stake, against $497/month for ReceptAI. **In outreach, ask for their numbers instead of asserting yours.**

**Derived figure (state it as an estimate):** One full-time CSR at the BLS median ($44,770 wages) costs roughly **$64,000/year fully loaded** if benefits follow the BLS private-industry average of 30% of total compensation ($44,770 ÷ 0.70). That covers about 40 hours of a 168-hour week, and nights and weekends need more people. ReceptAI at $497/month is about **$5,964/year**.
- Small contractors often offer fewer benefits than the private-industry average, so present the $64k as an estimate ("roughly", "up to"). The wage-only figure ($44,770) is the conservative, fully sourced number.

## 6. Google Local Services Ads (LSA): how it charges and what leads cost

**Charging (high confidence):**
- LSA charges per lead, not per click. Google's help page says: "Pay only for leads related to your business." It also says: "If you regularly fail to answer calls or respond to messages, your ad ranking may be affected." Source: https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/6224841 (Independent/government-grade: platform's own policy).
- **Effective October 1, 2026:** Google charges for **missed calls during business hours** when the caller stays on the line **more than 20 seconds**. For IVR systems the timer starts after the caller presses a routing key. **Follow-up calls** can be charged if the first call didn't qualify. Repeat contact within **15 days** is charged once. Source: Google's email to advertisers, reported by Search Engine Roundtable (Aug 25, 2026), https://www.seroundtable.com/google-lsa-missed-subsequent-calls-41940.html, and PPC Land, https://ppc.land/google-charges-local-services-advertisers-for-missed-calls-over-20-seconds/ (Industry body/publication). PPC Land notes Google had not published a standalone help doc and that details such as voicemail handling and disputes remain unclear.

**Cost per lead (all from LSA agencies, so Vendor marketing, use with caution):**

| Trade | CPL | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Electrical | $39 (SearchLight); $43 (99 Calls, Q2 2026) | BookedFriday citing SearchLight Feb 2026 ($6.72M spend, 888 contractors), https://bookedfriday.com/local-services-ads-cost ; 99 Calls, https://99calls.com/blog/lsa-cost-per-lead-by-industry |
| HVAC | $51–$80 | SearchLight via BookedFriday |
| Plumbing | $57–$69 (SearchLight); $66 (99 Calls) | as above |

- About **90%** of LSA leads arrive as phone calls (SearchLight via BookedFriday; vendor, unverified independently).
- Claims that "missed calls tank LSA rank" or that there is a numeric "phone response score" come from agencies (e.g. Tradesly, BookedFriday) and are **(unverified)**. Google's documented statement is only "your ad ranking may be affected."

## 9. Safe to quote (exact phrasing)

**Note:** "Safe" means the number is real and traceable, not that it's independent. Most items below come from companies that sell software or call answering, including competitors (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, CallRail, Invoca). Always name the source in the sentence. Only the BLS/O*NET figures and Google's own help-page wording are independent.

Use these sentences **verbatim or nearly so**, at most one or two stats per message.

1. **Google LSA missed-call billing:** "Since October 1, Google charges Local Services advertisers for missed calls during business hours when the caller stays on the line more than 20 seconds, according to Google's notice to advertisers reported by Search Engine Roundtable."
2. **Google LSA ranking:** "Google's own Local Services help page says that if you regularly fail to answer calls or respond to messages, your ad ranking may be affected."
3. **CSR cost (BLS):** "The BLS puts the median customer service rep wage at about $21.50 an hour, roughly $45,000 a year before benefits, and that covers about 40 of the 168 hours in a week."
4. **Dispatcher cost:** "Dispatchers earn a median of about $24 an hour, according to BLS data."
5. **Consumer behavior (attribute to CallRail):** "In CallRail's 2025 survey of 1,000 US consumers, 78% said they've given up on a business after an unanswered call."
6. **Unanswered rate (attribute to vendor):** "Call-tracking company Invoca reports that about a quarter of calls to home services businesses go unanswered."
7. **HVAC after-hours share (ServiceTitan):** "ServiceTitan data shows about 14% of residential HVAC calls came in outside business hours in June 2025, mostly between 5 and 9pm."
8. **Evening booking drop (ServiceTitan 2022):** "ServiceTitan found small shops booked only about 9% of calls that came in after 6pm."
9. **Booking gap (ServiceTitan 2026):** "ServiceTitan's data shows top-performing contractors book 62% of inbound lead calls versus 39% for everyone else."
10. **Emergency premiums (Angi):** "Angi's cost guide says emergency plumbing typically runs 1.5 to 3 times normal rates." / "Angi lists the average water damage restoration job at about $3,900."
11. **Customer expectations (Jobber):** "In Jobber's 2026 survey of home service businesses, over half said customers expect a response within the hour."
12. **AI acceptance (Housecall Pro):** "Housecall Pro's homeowner survey found 53% are comfortable with AI handling the first contact."
13. **Speed to lead (HBR):** "Harvard Business Review research found companies that responded to leads within an hour were about seven times more likely to qualify them." Say "leads", not "calls".

## 10. Do not quote

| Claim | Why |
|---|---|
| "62% of calls to small businesses go unanswered" | 411 Locals, about 2016: 85 businesses, cross-industry, counts voicemail as unanswered. It is routinely misattributed to recent years and to home services. |
| "85% of callers whose calls aren't answered won't call back" | No primary study found. Circulates among answering-service vendors (attributed to PATLive and others) without citation. |
| "80% of callers sent to voicemail hang up / don't leave a message" | Traced to a Forbes contributor/Tech Council piece and vendor blogs with no primary study. Contradicted by CallRail's 42% "leave a voicemail". |
| "Less than 3% of callers leave a voicemail" | Invoca, no methodology disclosed, and it conflicts with other vendor data. |
| "86% hang up at voicemail; 60% call a competitor within minutes" | PATLive blog, no source cited. |
| "Small businesses lose $126,000 a year to missed calls" | Vendor arithmetic (AMBS Call Center). No study behind it. |
| "78% of customers buy from the first business that responds" | Untraceable origin (often attributed to "Lead Connect"). |
| "30–48% of contractor calls come after hours" | No primary study. ServiceTitan's measured HVAC figure is about 10–14%. |
| "Phone leads convert 10–15x better than web leads" (BIA/Kelsey) | Claim reportedly absent from the cited report. |
| "Missed calls tank your LSA ranking" / "LSA phone response score" | Agency speculation. Use Google's actual wording (Safe #2) instead. |
| Any "you're losing $X per month" about a specific prospect | We don't know their numbers. Ask instead. |
| Pest control average ticket / LSA CPL for garage door, pest, locksmith | Not verified in this research. |
