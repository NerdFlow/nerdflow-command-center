## How to use proof in outreach

- **First email:** usually no proof, or one short line. The hook is their situation, not our résumé.
- **Day-3 follow-up:** best place for proof. Once live, the demo line is the default ("call [number] and pretend your AC died").
- **Replies to "does this actually work?":** demo line, then sample recording, then pilot offer.
- **Never** inflate: no "dozens of contractors use us," no invented numbers, no naming customers without permission.
