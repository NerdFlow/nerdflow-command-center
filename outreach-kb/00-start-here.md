# 00 — Start Here: How to Use This Knowledge Base

**Read this first.** This is the operating guide for any AI agent (Grok Bot, Sales Pipeline, Flow) that writes, sends, or answers outreach for NerdFlow. It is short on purpose. Read the full docs only when a task needs them.

---

## 1. The three layers

| Layer | Folder | Answers | Changes when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sales Craft** | `1 Sales Craft` | *How* to sell: writing, discovery, objections, price, closing, follow-up, tone, compliance | Rarely. Universal across products and markets |
| **Core** | `2 Core` | *What* we sell and what's true: Product Truth, Proof Bank, Message Principles, Universal Learnings | When the product or proof changes |
| **ICP modules** | `3 ICP – ...` | *Who* we sell to: buyer profiles, their language, signals, competitors, objections, market facts, results | Per market. A pivot = a new module |

Every outreach message or reply must combine all three: **Sales Craft** for how to say it, **Core** for what's true, and the **ICP module** for who it's for.

---

## 2. Order of authority (when docs conflict)

1. **Product Truth** — never claim anything it doesn't support. If it's silent, ask Muqeet. Do not guess.
2. **Ethics and compliance** (Sales Craft, section 10) — CAN-SPAM, TCPA, AI disclosure, no fake urgency.
3. **Approved learnings** (marked Approved in a Learnings doc)
4. **Message Principles**
5. Everything else in Sales Craft and the ICP module

---

## 3. Non-negotiable rules

1. **Never make a claim about ReceptAI that isn't in Product Truth.** Anything marked `[confirm in Product Truth]` in other docs is not usable until it's confirmed there.
2. **Never quote a statistic that isn't on the "Safe to quote" list** in the ICP's Market Facts doc, and always name the source.
3. **If someone asks whether they're talking to an AI, say yes.**
4. **Every cold email has a physical postal address and a clear opt-out.** Honor opt-outs immediately and log them.
5. **No AI-voice calls or automated texts to US numbers without Muqeet's explicit sign-off** (TCPA risk; see Sales Craft section 10).
6. **Every opener is based on a verified fact with a source link in the tracker.** No source, no send.
7. **Signature matches the sending mailbox.** Ryan sends, Ryan signs.
8. **Don't send.** Draft, queue, and flag. A human approves sends until Muqeet changes this rule.
9. **Quotes in Prospect Language are for learning how buyers talk.** Never paste them into outreach as if they were the prospect's own words, and never attribute them to a named person.

## 5. The learning loop

**Daily**
- Log every reply word for word in the Results Log: date, lead, ICP, mailbox, message version, reply tag (see Sales Craft §11).
- New objection or new prospect phrase → add to the ICP doc marked **Pending approval**.

**Weekly (Monday)**
- Report sends, reply rate, positive reply rate, meetings, pilots, conversions — by message version.
- Propose learnings in the Learnings doc with evidence. Do not edit Message Principles or Message Library until Muqeet marks a learning **Approved**.

**Monthly (first Monday)**
- Refresh ICP Competitors and Prospect Language research.

**Sample-size rules**
- Under 100 sends per version: no conclusions. Report numbers only.
- 100+ sends per version: you may flag a version that's clearly failing (e.g. 0 positive replies vs. several).
- To call a winner between close versions (say 3% vs 6% reply rate) you need roughly **750 sends per version**. Until then, label it "early signal."

**Promotion rules**
- Tag every learning **Universal** or **ICP-specific**.
- A learning becomes Universal only if it holds in 2+ ICPs, or Muqeet approves it.

---

## 6. Adding a new ICP (pivot or test)

1. Copy the `ICP Module Template` folder and rename it `3 ICP – [Market]`.
2. Research each template doc for the new market.
3. Reuse Sales Craft and Core as-is. Do not copy contractor-specific learnings.
4. Tag all leads and sends with the new ICP name.

## 7. Known gaps (as of October 2026)

- **Product Truth is empty.** The ReceptAI team must fill it before outreach claims integrations, booking, emergency handling or setup time.
- **Demo line is not live.** Proof Bank lists it as pending.
- **Reddit, Yelp and Google reviews were blocked** during research. Prospect Language comes from forums, trade press, podcasts and job posts that loaded. Many forum posts are years old: use them for how contractors talk, not as current facts.
- **Most missed-call stats come from vendors** and contradict each other. Market Facts rates every one.
- **Google Local Services missed-call charges (from October 1, 2026)** come from Google's notice to advertisers as reported by Search Engine Roundtable; Google hasn't published a help page yet. Quote it only with that source named, as in Market Facts.
- **Many "Safe to quote" stats come from vendors, including competitors** (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, CallRail, Invoca). Always name the source. Only BLS/O*NET and Google's own help-page wording are independent.
- **Some research docs describe what ReceptAI does as examples.** Each carries a warning banner: confirm in Product Truth before using any capability with a prospect.
