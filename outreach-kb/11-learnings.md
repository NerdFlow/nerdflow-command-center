# Learnings

**Rule for AI agents:** Propose learnings here; never apply them until Muqeet marks them **Approved**. Every learning needs evidence (numbers, sample size, or quoted replies) and a scope tag.

**Scope tags:** `Universal` (holds across ICPs; requires 2+ ICPs or Muqeet's approval) · `ICP: [name]` (only this market)

**Confidence (based on sends per version):**
- **Anecdote:** under 100 sends, or based on a few replies
- **Early signal:** 100–750 sends per version
- **Confirmed:** 750+ sends per version, or a large, consistent gap
