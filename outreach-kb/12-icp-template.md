# ICP Module Template

**How to use:** Copy this folder, rename it `3 ICP – [Market]`, and create one doc per section below. Research each one before any outreach to the new market. Sales Craft and Core are reused as-is; do not copy another ICP's learnings.

---

## Module docs to create

### 1. Overview
- Market name and definition (industry, geography, company size)
- Why this market (problem fit, budget, reachability)
- Primary offer for this market (must match Product Truth)
- Disqualifiers

### 2. Buyer Profiles
For each role (decision-maker, influencer, user, gatekeeper): their day, what they care about, how they buy, where they spend time, how they react to cold outreach, what proof they trust, best angle, likely objections.

### 3. Prospect Language
40+ real phrases the buyers use about the problem, grouped by theme, each with a source link. "Words they use / words to avoid" lists. Rules for using them.

### 4. Buying Signals and Lead Scoring
Observable public signals, how to find each (sites, search queries), strength, outreach angle. A 0–100 scoring model with disqualifiers. Timing by season/day.

### 5. Competitors
Direct competitors, substitutes, and "do nothing." Pricing with sources and dates. Where our price sits. How to talk about competitors.

### 6. Objection Library
Real objections for this market. For each: the concern behind it, email reply (<60 words), call reply (2–4 sentences + a question), what not to say. Anything depending on a product capability: `[confirm in Product Truth]`.

### 7. Market Facts
Stats with source, publisher, year and quality rating (Independent / Industry / Vendor). "Safe to quote" and "Do not quote" lists.

### 8. Message Versions
Subject lines, email 1, LinkedIn note, follow-ups — all passing the Quality Gate in Message Principles.

### 9. Results and Learnings
Tag all sends with this ICP. Use the Learnings template from Core.

---

## Research prompt for the agent

> Build a new ICP module for [market: industry, geography, company size]. Fill each of the 9 docs above using web research. Cite sources inline with URLs, mark anything unverified, rate every statistic by source quality, and mark any claim that depends on a product capability as [confirm in Product Truth]. Don't contact anyone. Show me a summary when done.
